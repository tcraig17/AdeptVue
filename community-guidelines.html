AdeptVue Community Guidelines

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Version: 1

AdeptVue is built for learning, teaching, sharing practical knowledge, and helping people improve their skills.

These Community Guidelines explain what is and is not allowed on AdeptVue. They apply to all users, including viewers, creators, comments, profiles, uploaded content, messages, and other activity on the Platform.

These Guidelines work together with AdeptVue’s:

  • Terms of Service
  • Privacy Policy
  • Creator Agreement, when applicable
  • the copyright and DMCA procedures in the Terms of Service

AdeptVue has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, loss of monetization, suspension, or account termination.

1. Respect Other People

AdeptVue does not allow targeted harassment, bullying, threats, or abusive behavior.

Do not:

  • threaten violence or serious harm;
  • repeatedly target someone with abusive or degrading comments;
  • encourage others to harass a person;
  • publish private information to intimidate or harm someone;
  • impersonate someone in a deceptive or harmful way;
  • use the Platform to stalk or intimidate another person.

Disagreement, criticism, and debate are allowed when they do not cross into targeted abuse or threats.

2. Hate and Dehumanizing Content

Do not attack, threaten, degrade, or dehumanize people based on protected characteristics such as:

  • race;
  • ethnicity;
  • national origin;
  • religion;
  • sex;
  • sexual orientation;
  • gender identity;
  • disability;
  • serious medical condition;
  • caste;
  • or another characteristic protected by applicable law.

Educational or documentary discussion of hateful conduct may be allowed when presented in context and not used to promote or endorse hatred.

3. Violence and Dangerous Conduct

Do not use AdeptVue to encourage, glorify, coordinate, or instruct people to commit violent or seriously harmful acts.

Content may be restricted or removed if it:

  • threatens violence;
  • celebrates serious real-world violence;
  • encourages dangerous stunts likely to cause serious injury;
  • teaches someone how to carry out unlawful violent conduct;
  • encourages others to harm themselves or another person.

Educational, historical, newsworthy, or safety-related discussion may be treated differently when context makes the purpose clear.

4. Self-Harm and Suicide

AdeptVue does not allow content that encourages, instructs, or glorifies suicide, self-harm, or eating-disorder behavior.

Supportive, recovery-focused, educational, or prevention-oriented discussion may be allowed.

AdeptVue may remove content or take safety-related action where there is a credible risk of serious harm.

5. Sexual Content and Nudity

AdeptVue is not an adult-content platform.

Do not upload:

  • pornography;
  • explicit sexual activity;
  • sexual solicitation;
  • sexualized nudity;
  • fetish content intended primarily for sexual gratification;
  • sexual exploitation;
  • non-consensual intimate imagery.

Limited non-sexual nudity may be allowed in legitimate educational, medical, artistic, or documentary contexts when appropriate.

6. Sexual Content Involving Minors

AdeptVue has zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation.

Do not upload, request, distribute, promote, or facilitate:

  • child sexual abuse material;
  • sexualized depictions of minors;
  • grooming;
  • sexual solicitation involving minors;
  • exploitation or trafficking of minors;
  • content that normalizes or encourages sexual abuse of minors.

AdeptVue may preserve and report relevant information to appropriate authorities where required or permitted by law.

7. Minor Safety

Users must not exploit, manipulate, endanger, or inappropriately contact minors.

This includes:

  • grooming behavior;
  • coercion;
  • predatory contact;
  • attempts to obtain sexual content;
  • requests for inappropriate private communication;
  • encouraging minors to conceal risky activity from parents or guardians;
  • dangerous challenges targeted toward minors.

Creators who are 16–17 are subject to additional Guardian-consent requirements and age-based protections.

8. Illegal Activity

Do not use AdeptVue to facilitate unlawful activity.

This includes using the Platform to:

  • plan crimes;
  • traffic illegal goods;
  • facilitate theft;
  • commit fraud;
  • evade law enforcement;
  • distribute stolen credentials;
  • facilitate cybercrime;
  • arrange illegal services;
  • encourage serious legal violations.

Educational discussion about crime, cybersecurity, history, law, or safety may be permitted when the purpose is legitimate and not instructional toward wrongdoing.

9. Fraud, Scams, and Deception

Do not use AdeptVue to deceive people for financial or personal gain.

Prohibited behavior includes:

  • investment scams;
  • fake giveaways;
  • phishing;
  • identity theft;
  • impersonation for fraud;
  • deceptive payment requests;
  • fraudulent fundraising;
  • fake job or business opportunities;
  • knowingly false claims designed to cause financial loss.

Creators must clearly distinguish legitimate sponsorships, promotions, and affiliate relationships from deceptive schemes.

10. Dangerous or Misleading Instructional Content

AdeptVue is designed for instructional and educational content, which means creators have a responsibility not to recklessly endanger viewers.

Do not knowingly present dangerous instructions as safe when they pose a substantial risk of serious injury, death, property damage, or unlawful conduct.

AdeptVue may restrict instructional content involving:

  • dangerous machinery;
  • electricity;
  • chemicals;
  • weapons;
  • hazardous materials;
  • medical procedures;
  • extreme physical activities;
  • other high-risk activities.

Safety warnings, qualifications, protective equipment, or professional-supervision notices may be required where appropriate.

11. Medical, Health, Legal, and Financial Content

Creators may discuss health, legal, financial, or other professional topics, but they must not knowingly misrepresent their qualifications or present materially misleading information.

Creators should clearly distinguish:

  • personal experience;
  • general education;
  • professional advice;
  • sponsored recommendations.

AdeptVue may require disclaimers or restrict content in sensitive or regulated categories.

12. Intellectual Property

Only upload content that you created or have the right to use.

Do not knowingly infringe:

  • copyright;
  • trademarks;
  • music rights;
  • video rights;
  • photography rights;
  • software rights;
  • publicity rights;
  • other intellectual-property rights.

Giving credit does not automatically give you permission to use someone else’s work.

AdeptVue may remove content in response to valid rights complaints.

13. Privacy and Personal Information

Do not publish another person’s sensitive private information without authorization.

This may include:

  • home address;
  • private phone number;
  • private email;
  • financial information;
  • government identification numbers;
  • medical information;
  • login credentials;
  • precise location;
  • other sensitive information.

Publicly available or professionally relevant information may be treated differently depending on context.

14. Impersonation

Do not impersonate another person, business, organization, or public figure in a way that is likely to deceive users.

Parody, commentary, or fan accounts may be allowed when clearly identified as such.

15. Misinformation and Material Deception

AdeptVue does not require every opinion or factual claim to be correct.

However, users must not knowingly use the Platform to spread materially deceptive information in ways likely to cause serious harm, fraud, or unsafe behavior.

AdeptVue may add context, reduce distribution, or remove content where necessary to protect users or comply with law.

16. Spam

Do not flood the Platform with unwanted or repetitive content.

Spam may include:

  • repetitive comments;
  • mass promotional posting;
  • misleading links;
  • copied content posted repeatedly;
  • automated engagement;
  • irrelevant promotional replies;
  • coordinated spam campaigns.

17. Artificial Engagement

Do not buy, sell, generate, manipulate, or coordinate fake:

  • followers;
  • views;
  • watch time;
  • Helpful activity;
  • comments;
  • saves;
  • shares;
  • subscriptions;
  • tips;
  • ad impressions;
  • Creator eligibility metrics.

AdeptVue may remove artificial engagement from analytics and monetization calculations.

18. Bots and Automation

Automated tools may not be used to manipulate the Platform, harass users, scrape restricted data, or create artificial activity.

Authorized integrations, accessibility tools, and approved automation may be permitted.

19. Account Evasion

Do not create or use additional accounts to evade:

  • bans;
  • suspensions;
  • age restrictions;
  • Guardian-consent requirements;
  • monetization restrictions;
  • moderation actions.

AdeptVue may restrict related accounts where evasion is reasonably established.

20. False Age or Identity Information

Users must provide truthful age and identity information where required.

Do not:

  • falsify date of birth;
  • falsely claim to be an adult;
  • use another person as a fake Guardian;
  • submit fraudulent identity documents;
  • bypass age-based restrictions.

Violations may result in loss of Creator access, monetization, or the Account itself.

21. Creator Responsibility

Creators are responsible for the content they publish and should make a reasonable effort to ensure instructional content is:

  • understandable;
  • appropriately contextualized;
  • not intentionally deceptive;
  • not unlawfully copied;
  • not recklessly dangerous.

Creators should correct material errors when they become aware of them where correction is reasonably appropriate.

22. Sponsorships and Affiliate Content

Creators must clearly disclose material commercial relationships when required.

This includes:

  • sponsorships;
  • paid endorsements;
  • free products;
  • affiliate links;
  • commissions;
  • brand relationships.

Disclosures should be clear and difficult to miss.

23. Selling Products or Services

If AdeptVue later permits creators to promote products or services, such activity must comply with:

  • applicable law;
  • Platform policies;
  • advertising rules;
  • consumer-protection laws;
  • disclosure requirements.

Prohibited products or services may not be promoted.

24. Regulated Goods and Services

AdeptVue may prohibit or restrict content involving regulated or high-risk goods and services.

This may include content facilitating unlawful or unsafe sales involving:

  • firearms;
  • controlled substances;
  • prescription medications;
  • tobacco or nicotine products;
  • alcohol;
  • gambling;
  • financial products;
  • adult services;
  • hazardous materials.

Educational discussion may be permitted depending on context.

25. Malware and Cyber Abuse

Do not use AdeptVue to distribute or facilitate:

  • malware;
  • ransomware;
  • credential theft;
  • phishing;
  • unauthorized system access;
  • destructive code;
  • malicious exploits.

Legitimate cybersecurity education, defensive research, and authorized testing may be allowed.

26. Graphic or Disturbing Content

Graphic content may be restricted where it primarily exists to shock, glorify violence, or exploit victims.

Educational, documentary, medical, or newsworthy content may be allowed with appropriate context or warnings.

27. Animal Abuse

Do not upload content depicting or encouraging deliberate animal cruelty or abuse.

Legitimate veterinary, agricultural, educational, or documentary content may be treated differently.

28. Harassment Through Comments or Replies

Comments and replies are subject to the same rules as uploaded videos.

Users may not use comments to:

  • threaten;
  • harass;
  • spam;
  • impersonate;
  • dox;
  • sexually solicit;
  • coordinate abuse.

29. Manipulative Creator Practices

Creators may not intentionally mislead users through deceptive platform tactics such as:

  • fake claims of guaranteed earnings;
  • false giveaways;
  • misleading thumbnails designed to facilitate scams;
  • fabricated testimonials;
  • deceptive calls for payment;
  • coordinated manipulation of engagement.

30. Platform Integrity

Do not interfere with AdeptVue’s technical systems.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • exploiting vulnerabilities;
  • bypassing rate limits;
  • abusing APIs;
  • attempting unauthorized access;
  • scraping restricted information;
  • interfering with content delivery;
  • attacking Platform infrastructure;
  • manipulating recommendation systems through prohibited means.

31. Creator Monetization Abuse

Creators participating in monetization programs may not:

  • generate fake views;
  • self-fund or coordinate fraudulent tips;
  • create fraudulent subscriptions;
  • manipulate advertising impressions;
  • intentionally exploit payout systems;
  • create false engagement rings;
  • use stolen payment methods.

Invalid activity may be excluded from earnings.

32. Enforcement

Depending on the nature, severity, frequency, and context of a violation, AdeptVue may take actions such as:

  • warning the user;
  • removing content;
  • limiting content distribution;
  • disabling comments;
  • restricting recommendations;
  • restricting Creator Features;
  • disabling monetization;
  • removing artificial engagement;
  • restricting account functionality;
  • temporarily suspending an Account;
  • permanently terminating an Account.

Serious violations may result in immediate action without prior warning.

33. Context Matters

AdeptVue may consider context when reviewing content.

Content that would otherwise violate these Guidelines may sometimes be permitted when clearly presented for legitimate:

  • educational;
  • documentary;
  • historical;
  • scientific;
  • artistic;
  • medical;
  • journalistic;
  • public-interest;
  • safety-related purposes.

Context does not automatically make harmful content acceptable.

34. Repeated Violations

Repeated violations may result in progressively stronger enforcement.

AdeptVue may consider:

  • severity;
  • frequency;
  • prior warnings;
  • user intent;
  • actual harm;
  • attempts to evade enforcement;
  • account history.

35. Appeals

Where AdeptVue provides an appeal process, users may request review of certain enforcement decisions.

An appeal does not guarantee reversal.

AdeptVue may consider:

  • additional context;
  • evidence;
  • policy application;
  • technical errors;
  • mistaken identity;
  • changed circumstances.

36. Emergency Situations

AdeptVue may take immediate action when reasonably necessary to respond to:

  • credible threats;
  • child exploitation;
  • serious self-harm risk;
  • imminent violence;
  • serious fraud;
  • significant security threats;
  • legal emergencies.

AdeptVue may preserve or disclose information where permitted or required by law.

37. Cooperation With Authorities

AdeptVue may cooperate with lawful investigations and legal requests consistent with applicable law and the Privacy Policy.

AdeptVue may report serious unlawful conduct where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users or others.

38. Community Standards May Evolve

These Guidelines may change as AdeptVue develops new features and learns from real-world use.

Material changes may be communicated through the Platform, website, email, or another appropriate method.

39. Reporting Violations

Users can report suspected violations through available Platform reporting tools.

For serious safety concerns, users may also contact:

support@adeptvue.com

Creator-related concerns may be sent to:

creators@adeptvue.com

Intellectual-property complaints should be sent to:

copyright@adeptvue.com

Copyright complaints should be sent to support@adeptvue.com and are handled under the copyright and DMCA procedures in the Terms of Service.
