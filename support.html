AdeptVue Support

This page explains how to contact AdeptVue, report a safety concern, and delete your account. AdeptVue is operated by AdeptVue, LLC, a Missouri limited liability company.

Contact support

For help with the AdeptVue app or a question about your account, email support@adeptvue.com.

For general questions about AdeptVue or the launch waitlist, you can also email hello@adeptvue.com.

Report a safety concern or objectionable content

If you see content or behavior that violates AdeptVue’s rules, you can report it in the AdeptVue app. On a video or Quick Tip, use the in-app report option and submit the report. You can also block a creator in the app so their content is hidden from you.

You may also email a safety concern or a report of objectionable content to support@adeptvue.com. Include enough detail for us to find the content or account, such as a username, video, or description of what happened.

AdeptVue has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. AdeptVue may remove violating content and may suspend or terminate accounts that violate the Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.

Delete your account

You can delete your AdeptVue account in the app: open Account Settings and select Delete account.

If you cannot use the in-app option, email support@adeptvue.com to request deletion.

FAQ

How do I contact support?

Email support@adeptvue.com.

How do I report objectionable content?

Use the in-app report option on a video or Quick Tip, or email support@adeptvue.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the AdeptVue app, go to Account Settings and select Delete account.
