AdeptVue Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

AdeptVue, LLC, doing business as AdeptVue (“AdeptVue,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), is a Missouri limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information on this website and the AdeptVue waitlist.

This page is written for the pre-launch waitlist site. It does not describe a future mobile app in detail. When the AdeptVue app is offered to the public, AdeptVue will publish an updated Privacy Policy that covers the app, and that policy will apply to app use.

This Privacy Policy is not legal advice.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed in connection with:

  • this AdeptVue website; and
  • the AdeptVue launch waitlist and related email updates.

It does not govern independent third-party websites or services that have their own privacy policies, including your email provider if you contact us by email.

2. Who this waitlist is for

The AdeptVue waitlist is intended for people who are at least thirteen (13) years old. Do not submit an email address if you are under thirteen. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under thirteen in circumstances where collection is not legally permitted, we will take steps to delete it as required by applicable law.

3. Information we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you provide. If you use the on-page form before a waitlist service is connected, your device may open a pre-filled message to hello@adeptvue.com through your own email application; in that case, your email provider also processes the message.

If you email us, we will receive the email address you send from and any information you include in the message.

When you visit this website, our hosting provider may automatically receive standard technical information that web servers typically log, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the date and time of the request. This site is not set up to use advertising cookies or analytics trackers.

We do not ask for a password, payment information, or a profile in order to join the waitlist.

4. How we use this information

We use waitlist email addresses to:

  • confirm that you asked to join the waitlist, if we send a confirmation;
  • email you when the AdeptVue app is ready to download; and
  • send related launch updates about AdeptVue.

We use information in messages you send us to read and respond to your request. We may use technical logs to operate, secure, and troubleshoot this website.

We do not sell your email address, and we do not use waitlist emails for unrelated advertising.

5. How we share information

We may share waitlist information with service providers who help us operate the website or send waitlist email, and only as needed for those purposes. We may also disclose information if we believe we are required to do so by law, to protect AdeptVue or others, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets.

If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours will process that message.

6. Retention

We keep waitlist email addresses until you ask to be removed, or until we no longer need them for the launch-update purpose described above. We may keep a record of your removal request. Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operations, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to be removed from the waitlist, or ask questions about this Privacy Policy, by emailing privacy@adeptvue.com or hello@adeptvue.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law, including to request access to or deletion of personal information we hold about you. We will consider those requests as required by law.

8. Children

This waitlist is not directed to children under thirteen (13), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under thirteen.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including when the AdeptVue app launches and a broader policy is needed. The revised Privacy Policy will identify its updated effective date. Where applicable law requires consent to a new practice, AdeptVue will obtain that consent before relying on the new practice.
