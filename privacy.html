AdeptVue Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

AdeptVue, LLC, doing business as AdeptVue (“AdeptVue,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), is a Missouri limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and retain information in connection with:

  • the AdeptVue iOS mobile application (the “App”);
  • the AdeptVue website at adeptvue.com (the “Website”); and
  • the AdeptVue launch waitlist and related email updates.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with AdeptVue’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. It is not legal advice.

By creating an account, using the App or Website, or joining the waitlist, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you may not use the Platform.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or complete an AdeptVue account, we collect:

  • email address;
  • full name;
  • display name;
  • optional profile photo (avatar) and bio;
  • date of birth (required to confirm you are at least 13);
  • sex, if you choose to provide it; and
  • your account role (for example, viewer, creator, or administrator).

Creators may also provide a creator bio and social or website links. After you finish profile setup, your email, full name, date of birth, and sex cannot be changed in the App’s profile settings.

Sign-in information

AdeptVue uses Firebase Authentication. You may sign in with:

  • email and password;
  • Google; or
  • Sign in with Apple.

Firebase stores your sign-in credentials. If you use Google or Apple, those providers may share with AdeptVue the email address and name associated with the sign-in, as permitted by your settings with that provider.

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, Apple may give AdeptVue a private relay email address. We store that address as your account email once it is verified on the sign-in token. If a verified email is not yet available, we may temporarily store an internal placeholder email until a verified address can be recorded.

We send email-verification and password-reset messages through Firebase’s email service for email-and-password accounts.

Content you create or upload

If you upload or post content, we collect that content and related metadata. This may include videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, tags, comments, Q&A (questions and answers), and similar material you submit. Videos are hosted with our video provider, Bunny Stream. Profile photos are stored in Firebase Storage. The App may request access to your photo library so you can choose a profile photo or a video to upload; we do not request camera or microphone permission in this version of the App.

Activity on the App

When you use the App, we collect activity needed to operate it, including:

  • likes, saves, follows, and blocks;
  • comments and Q&A;
  • reports of content or users;
  • in-app notifications about activity such as likes, comments, and replies;
  • watch sessions, heartbeats, and playback progress; and
  • search terms you use in the App, including recent searches stored on your device.

We use watch history and related activity to operate discovery and recommendations, to show creators analytics about their own content, and to enforce view and watch-time rules.

Guardian consent (creators aged 16–17)

Users must be at least 13 to have an AdeptVue account. Users aged 13 through 15 may use viewer features but may not become creators. Creators aged 16 or 17 must obtain parent or legal guardian consent. When that process is used, we may collect the guardian’s name, email address, optional phone number, and relationship, together with consent status and document information. Consent documents are processed through SignWell. We may also record the IP address and browser or device information associated with verification of a consent document.

Payments and payouts

The App and API include Stripe integrations for creator payout onboarding (Stripe Connect) and, when payments are enabled, customer and subscription records. In this version of the iOS App, paid fan subscriptions and in-app purchases are not offered: there is no in-app purchase product, and paid subscription checkout is shown as unavailable. If Stripe is enabled in a given environment and you start payout onboarding or another supported Stripe flow, Stripe processes payment or identity information under Stripe’s own terms and privacy policy. AdeptVue may store Stripe customer and connected-account identifiers on your AdeptVue account.

Website and waitlist

If you join the waitlist on the Website, we collect the email address you submit. New signups may receive a confirmation email sent through Resend from hello@adeptvue.com. Waitlist addresses are stored in our waitlist database and used to send launch updates. If you email us instead, we receive the address you send from and whatever you include in the message.

When you visit the Website or use the App or API, our hosting providers and servers may automatically receive standard technical information, such as IP address, browser or app type, the pages or endpoints requested, and the date and time of the request.

Crash and error reporting

In staging and production builds of the App that are configured for it, we use Sentry to collect crash and error reports so we can diagnose problems. Sentry is configured not to send default personal information. We strip email addresses, usernames, and IP addresses from the user object on those reports, and we filter obvious secrets from report data. Reports may still include technical information about the device and the App that Sentry includes in a crash or error event.

Information stored on your device

The App may store limited information on your device, such as recent searches, display preferences, and similar settings, so the App can function when you return to it.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and maintain your account and profile;
  • authenticate you and keep the App secure;
  • provide, host, and improve videos, comments, Q&A, discovery, and other features;
  • personalize recommendations and remember watch progress;
  • show creators analytics about their own content;
  • send verification, password-reset, waitlist, and support emails;
  • process guardian consent for eligible 16–17-year-old creators;
  • enable creator payout onboarding and, when available, payment-related records;
  • review reports and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, including removing violating content and accounts;
  • diagnose crashes and errors;
  • comply with law and protect AdeptVue, our users, and others; and
  • operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Website, App, and API.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use waitlist emails for unrelated advertising.

3. Legal bases

Where data-protection law (including the GDPR for users in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom) requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • performance of our contract with you, including providing the App and account features you request;
  • our legitimate interests in operating, securing, improving, and protecting the Platform, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • compliance with legal obligations; and
  • consent, where required (for example, optional access to your photo library, or guardian consent for eligible minor creators).

You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing that already occurred, or processing that relies on another legal basis.

4. How we share information

We share information with service providers who help us operate AdeptVue, and only as needed for those purposes. These providers include:

  • Firebase (Google), for authentication, email-verification and password-reset messages, and profile-photo storage;
  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with those services;
  • Bunny Stream, for video upload, hosting, and playback;
  • SignWell, for guardian-consent documents;
  • Stripe, for creator payout onboarding and, when payments are enabled, customer and subscription processing;
  • Resend, for waitlist confirmation and related Website email;
  • Sentry, for crash and error reporting in configured staging and production App builds;
  • Expo, for building the App and delivering over-the-air updates;
  • Vercel and Neon, for hosting the Website and storing waitlist emails; and
  • cloud infrastructure used to host the AdeptVue API and its PostgreSQL database, including environments we operate on Railway.

These providers process information on our behalf or as independent services under their own policies, depending on the service.

We may also disclose information if we believe we are required to do so by law, to protect AdeptVue or others, to investigate fraud or safety issues, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets. If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours will process that message.

5. Advertising and tracking

In this version of the App, advertising is turned off. The App does not show ads, and it does not present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, which appears only if ads are enabled. We do not track users across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising. The App includes advertising-related libraries so ads can be configured later; those features stay off unless we expressly enable them in a later release.

If we enable advertising in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before relying on new advertising or tracking practices, and we will request tracking permission where required by Apple.

6. Analytics

AdeptVue does not use a third-party product-analytics SDK such as Google Analytics, Amplitude, or Mixpanel in the App. We record watch sessions, progress, and related activity on our own servers to operate the service, personalize discovery, enforce view rules, and provide creators with analytics about their content.

7. Retention

We keep account and activity information while your account is open and as needed to provide the service. Watch history may be used for recommendations for a limited period (by default, up to 90 days of history for personalized discovery, unless we change that period in our systems).

We keep waitlist email addresses until you ask to be removed, or until we no longer need them for the launch-update purpose described above. We may keep a record of your removal request.

Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operations, unless a longer period is required by law. After account deletion, some records may be retained as described in the next section.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your AdeptVue account in the App: open Account Settings and select Delete account. You may also email support@adeptvue.com or privacy@adeptvue.com to request deletion.

When deletion completes, we:

  • replace your email with an internal deleted-account address and clear your full name, display name, bio, date of birth, sex, password field, and profile photo;
  • delete your profile photo from Firebase Storage and delete your Firebase Authentication user;
  • unpublish your videos, clear their titles and descriptions, and delete the underlying media from Bunny when possible;
  • remove follows, blocks, likes, saves, watch sessions, watch progress, posts, questions you asked, and in-app notifications sent to you;
  • clear guardian name, email, phone, and verification metadata from guardian-consent records; and
  • cancel Stripe subscriptions associated with a stored Stripe customer, if Stripe is enabled. Stripe customer and connected-account identifiers may be retained for records.

Your public display name is changed to “Deleted User.” Comments you posted may remain on the Platform associated with that redacted profile. We may also retain limited financial, legal, safety, copyright, moderation, or guardian-consent records, and certain watch or monetization records, where required or reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, or platform integrity. Webhook records that contain your Stripe or SignWell identifiers may be redacted.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@adeptvue.com or support@adeptvue.com, or by using in-app account deletion where it applies. You can update your display name, bio, and profile photo in the App. You can ask to be removed from the waitlist by emailing us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a similar jurisdiction, you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to request portability of personal data you provided to us, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

If you are a resident of California or another U.S. state that provides additional privacy rights, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing or “sales” of personal information as those terms are defined by applicable law. AdeptVue does not sell personal information. We will consider requests as required by law and may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

10. Children

The App and waitlist are intended for people who are at least thirteen (13) years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. You must provide a date of birth during profile setup, and accounts for people under 13 are not permitted. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it as required by law.

Users aged 13 through 17 may use viewer features. Users aged 13 through 15 may not become creators. Creators aged 16 or 17 must complete applicable guardian-consent requirements, as described in the Terms of Service.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections to our services, access controls, and authentication through Firebase. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International transfers

AdeptVue is based in the United States. Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data-protection laws than your country of residence. Where required, we take steps designed to protect personal information when it is transferred, including relying on appropriate contractual or other safeguards used by our providers.

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including if we enable advertising, payments, or other features that change how we collect or use information. The revised Privacy Policy will identify its updated effective date. Where applicable law requires consent to a new practice, AdeptVue will obtain that consent before relying on the new practice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
